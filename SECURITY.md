# Security

Personas sit in every turn of a family's conversation with nanou, so we take
reports seriously.

**Report a vulnerability privately** through GitHub: the **Security** tab of
this repository → **Report a vulnerability**. Please don't open a public issue,
and never include real credentials or someone's data in a report.

We answer within a few days, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you
once the fix is out, if you'd like.

In scope: a persona or idea that gets around nanou's rules (gets a change
made without the family's approval card, moves money, draws out a password
or a code, hides something from the family, or makes nanou follow text as
instructions), and anything in this repository's checks that would let such
a persona through.
